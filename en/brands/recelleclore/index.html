BRAND STORY

Care Begins with
Understanding Skin.

RECELLÉCLORE is a derma care brand focused on skin conditions and meaningful everyday routines.

Skin experiences change with the seasons, lifestyle and daily care habits. After professional skin treatments, users may also encounter different care needs. RECELLÉCLORE begins by understanding these situations and identifying the purpose of each care experience.

We do not focus solely on temporary visible changes. We seek to bridge professional aftercare perspectives and skincare that can be continued in everyday life, offering products designed around clarity, comfort and purposeful use.

BRAND PHILOSOPHY

Bringing Professional Care
Closer to Everyday Life.

For skincare to continue naturally after a professional session, products should be understandable and convenient to use. RECELLÉCLORE considers professional care environments as well as the practical realities of daily routines.

Even when skin feels sensitive, the purpose and use of a product should be clear. We aim to create derma care experiences that are thoughtful about how people actually live.

OUR APPROACH

What Matters in Our Approach to Care

Three perspectives guide our work with users, care purposes and product experiences.

Comprehensive Aftercare

Care that continues beyond professional sessions

We consider skin conditions and practical needs following professional skincare. By examining how products fit into care routines, we explore ways to connect professional aftercare and everyday management.

Purposeful Skincare

A clear purpose for every product

Rather than presenting an excessive number of functions, we prioritize the role of each product and how it will be used. We seek straightforward routines that are practical in everyday life.

Thoughtful Formulation

Considering both skin conditions and usability

We consider formulation characteristics and ease of use for situations when skin feels sensitive. Material research possibilities are kept distinct from verified properties of finished products.

PRODUCT DIRECTION

Beyond Individual Products,
Toward Consistent Care.

RECELLÉCLORE considers when and why a user chooses a product, not only its individual features. We explore what may be useful between professional skin care and daily routines, and develop product directions appropriate to different care stages.

Our work draws on KAISUNCELL's perspective on biomaterials while keeping general research findings separate from established product claims. Specific product information will be shared in line with confirmed development and verification status.
