BRAND PHILOSOPHY

Connecting professional care with daily routines.

CLRX is KAISUNCELL's specialized care brand built around different scalp and skin care environments.

Care needs vary by purpose, condition and setting. CLRX focuses on understanding the user's routine, from everyday scalp care to the period following professional procedures, and developing practical product experiences.

Drawing on KAISUNCELL's work in marine-derived biomaterials and product development, the brand evaluates material applications alongside usability and real-world care requirements.

CARE AREAS

Three areas of care

01

Scalp Care

Everyday and professional scalp care

We consider scalp and hair conditions, daily habits and product usability to explore convenient scalp care routines.

02

Post-Procedure Care

Care following professional services

We examine the changing requirements after professional care and focus on continuity, comfort and ease of use.

03

Barrier Care

Skin barrier care

We explore practical care needs for skin that may feel dry or sensitive, with an emphasis on sustainable daily routines.

RESEARCH-LED CARE

From material research to meaningful experiences

KAISUNCELL researches applications of biomaterials including Water-Soluble CTSF™. CLRX explores relevant product concepts; claims for individual products will be communicated based on their own validation.
