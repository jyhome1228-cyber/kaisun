OUR PERSPECTIVE

Rethinking the value of resources

Material innovation starts by reconsidering how existing resources can be used.

Marine resources serve many industries, often generating by-products in the process. Viewing those by-products as potential raw materials rather than only waste opens new opportunities.

Chitosan is a naturally derived polymer obtainable from sources including crustaceans. KAISUNCELL considers how marine-derived materials may become suitable for professional care applications.

Natural origin alone does not establish environmental superiority. We believe material sourcing, use and product development should be considered together, with claims based on verifiable information.
