WHO WE ARE

Bio Innovation for Better Care.

New care possibilities inspired by biotechnology.

KAISUNCELL is a bio-healthcare company developing specialized care products and brands for hair loss, scalp and skin care. Drawing on CTSol-CLRX™ material technology associated with Kai-i Company, we connect research with product planning and experiences spanning professional and everyday care. We believe in closing the gap between technical possibilities and real-world use. By recognizing the different needs of each care field, we bring together research, product planning, brand development and business collaboration.

OUR BUSINESS

What We Do.

Turning technology into product and brand value.

Material Applications

We explore the properties and application environments of marine-derived water-soluble chitosan materials. For each care category, we refine potential uses and make material research a starting point for product development.

Product Development

We consider the distinct environments of hair loss, scalp and skin care when developing products for specific purposes. Our aim is to balance ease of everyday use with the needs of professional care settings.

Brand Commercialization

We give each brand a clear focus and a meaningful user experience. Through professional channels and distribution partnerships, we seek sustainable ways to bring relevant products to market.

EXPERTISE

Research Meets Real-World Care.

Connecting research, products and markets.

KAISUNCELL takes an R&D-oriented view of materials, product formats and real-world conditions. We examine appropriate applications and intended purposes based on professional care needs. Potential at the research stage should not be mistaken for proven product efficacy; we develop our direction using verified information. In this process, we aim to strengthen the links among materials, products and brands while expanding opportunities for research and business partnerships.

VISION & MISSION

Our Direction.

Advancing specialized care through lasting technological value.

Vision

Our vision is to connect biomaterial possibilities with better experiences in specialized hair loss, scalp and skin care. We take a long-term view of how research-informed understanding can become meaningful value for products and brands.

Mission

Building on material understanding, we plan products suited to each care category and develop brands around user experience. We connect research, distribution and business partnerships to help technology-informed products reach the markets and people who need them.
