About KAISUNCELL

Research, product development and brand commercialization.

Who We Are

Bio Innovation for Better Care

KAISUNCELL is a bio-healthcare company developing specialized products and brands for scalp, skin and oral care. Drawing on Water-Soluble CTSF™ material technology associated with Kai-i Company, we connect material research with practical product planning and business development.

Our Business

Material Applications

We examine the characteristics of marine-derived biomaterials and their suitability for different care environments. Our approach considers intended use, material potential and product experience.

Product Development

From Research to Products

Care environments differ across skin, scalp and oral applications. We consider usage conditions and convenience while developing product concepts.

Vision & Mission

Our Direction

Our vision is to bring the potential of biomaterials into better professional care. Our mission is to connect research-informed product development, focused brands and responsible commercial partnerships.
