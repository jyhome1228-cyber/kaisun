Stability in Hydrated Conditions

Water-soluble materials may change shape and properties when exposed to moisture. Understanding the desired behavior under real use conditions is important.

Comparing Crosslinking Approaches

Physical and chemical crosslinking represent different approaches to polymer structure. Each has benefits and considerations depending on its use; neither is inherently optimal in all settings.

Evaluation Considerations

Evaluations should consider use environment, duration and testing methods. General material properties and finished-product performance require separate verification.
