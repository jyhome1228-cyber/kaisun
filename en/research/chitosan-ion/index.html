Chitosan as a Natural Polymer

Chitosan is a naturally derived polymer researched in various biomaterial fields. Solubility and dispersibility may vary with molecular properties and conditions.

The Role of Molecular Weight

Molecular weight is one factor influencing material behavior. Research discusses possible changes in dissolution and diffusion, but outcomes depend on conditions.

Understanding Ionic Properties

Chitosan can exhibit charge-related characteristics depending on its environment. These may influence interactions with other materials but do not establish clinical effects.
