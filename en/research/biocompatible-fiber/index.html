Biocompatibility Considerations for Water-Soluble Fibers

What matters when evaluating materials intended for contact with the body? This article considers usage environments and biocompatibility evaluation.

OVERVIEW

What matters when evaluating materials intended for contact with the body? This article considers usage environments and biocompatibility evaluation.

Understanding Biocompatibility

Biocompatibility concerns how a material responds in its intended contact environment. Evaluations differ by contact site, duration and use purpose. General material properties cannot establish the safety of a finished product.

Use Conditions for Water-Soluble Materials

Water-soluble fibers interact with moisture. Product development should consider hydration, the method of use, and potential contact with skin or mucosal surfaces.

Implications for Product Development

KAISUNCELL evaluates potential applications in professional care. General considerations of biocompatibility must be distinguished from product-specific testing, certification and verification.
