Concentration–Viscosity Relationships and Formulation Stability

Understanding how concentration and viscosity relate to formulation feel and stability, with emphasis on actual usage conditions.

OVERVIEW

Concentration and Physical Properties

Changes in polymer concentration can affect viscosity, dispersibility and other material properties. The relationships depend on material characteristics and environmental conditions rather than a single universal formula.

Is Higher Viscosity Always Better?

Viscosity can influence product feel, application and uniformity. A higher value is not inherently preferable; an appropriate balance depends on the intended purpose and use environment.

Differences Between Skin and Scalp

Skin and scalp differ in moisture, sebum and the presence of hair. Product development should consider the body area and method of use, even when evaluating similar formulations. Understanding material behavior can help improve usability.

Product Development Perspective

KAISUNCELL examines material applications with product format and usage conditions in mind. This article presents general considerations from public research, without disclosing formulations or manufacturing know-how.
