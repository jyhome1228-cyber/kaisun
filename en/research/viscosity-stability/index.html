OVERVIEW

Understanding how concentration and viscosity relate to formulation properties and usability.

Concentration and Physical Properties

Changes in polymer concentration may affect viscosity, dispersibility and other properties. The relationships depend on material characteristics and environmental conditions.

Is Higher Viscosity Always Better?

Viscosity affects feel, spreadability and product stability. Higher viscosity is not automatically better; an appropriate balance depends on intended use.

Differences Between Skin and Scalp

Skin and scalp differ in moisture, sebum and the presence of hair. Even similar formulations require consideration of the body area and method of use.
