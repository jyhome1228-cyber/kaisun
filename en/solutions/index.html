Senior & Barrier Care

Understanding changing skin needs across age and environment

Skin care requirements differ according to age, climate, lifestyle and personal skin conditions. Especially for dry or sensitive-feeling skin, products should be comfortable and practical for ongoing use.

KAISUNCELL examines product possibilities for senior and skin barrier care. We consider material applications and user convenience across different environments. Specific products and launch information will be shared once confirmed.

Oral Care

A new everyday approach to oral care

Oral care is an ongoing habit rather than something needed only at particular moments. Since needs differ with age, lifestyle and personal routines, accessible product experiences matter.
